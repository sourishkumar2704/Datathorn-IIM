# Data & Source Registry

This document contains the complete source registry used in the Stage 1 research proposal.

---

## S01 — PIB MSME Economic Context

**Role:** Indian economic context

**Purpose:** Provides high-level context regarding the economic importance of MSMEs in India.

**Can tell us:**
- MSME economic contribution
- employment context
- registered enterprise context

**Cannot tell us:**
- Individual business sales
- Individual business cash flow
- Individual business forecasts

**Official source:**

https://www.pib.gov.in/PressNoteDetails.aspx?ModuleId=3&NoteId=157367&lang=2&reg=3

## S08 — ASUSE

**Role:** Indian business context

**Potential use:** Establishment-level Indian business context including activity, employment, expenses and receipts.

**Limitation:** Not a substitute for individual business-level daily forecasting data.

**Official source:**

https://esankhyiki.mospi.gov.in/catalogue-main/catalogue?page=1&product=ASUSE&rowsPerPage=10

## S10 — ONDC Open Data

**Role:** Experimental ecosystem signal

**Potential use:** Aggregate network activity such as orders, sellers, geography and trends.

**Official source:**

https://opendata.ondc.org/retail

## S11 — GST GSTR-1

**Role:** Potential business-level sales/invoice evidence

**Potential use:** Outward-supply/invoice information where legitimately available.

**Important limitation:**

GST invoice information is not equivalent to cash received.

**GST ≠ cash flow**

**Official source:**

https://tutorial.gst.gov.in/userguide/returns/Creation_of_Outward_Supplies_Return_in_GSTR-1.htm
